BlockWeeks 1–8
Flexible by Design: This program is a framework, not a mandate. Adjust intent, volume, and session timing based on how you feel each day. If something feels off — lower the intent or volume. Communicate everything. The program adapts. You lead.
Coaches Note — Mound Integration: As the program progresses, we will move some plyo work and catch-play to the mound with coaches discretion. This will be communicated week to week — do not make that adjustment without confirmation.
Goals & Program Structure
Pitcher Goals
Throw harder — develop real, sustainable velocity
Throw more in the zone — velo with command, not velo instead of command
Learn yourself — mechanically and mentally. Know who you are as a pitcher.
Build the body that supports the arm — strength, stability, and durability
Block Structure
Weeks 1–3: M/W/F throwing — plyo foundation, building to 120ft
Week 4–5: 4-day throw (M/T/T/F) — building distance and intent at 120ft+
Weeks 6–7: Pulldown phase — 3 then 5 pulldowns at max intent
Week 8: Deload — mandatory. Week 7 catches off the mound.
Program Focus Areas
01
Velocity Development
Plyo progressions, pulldowns, and long toss build real arm speed. Command comes from repeating the pattern that produces velocity.
02
Arm Care
Arm care A on Tuesday, B on Friday — to feel. Don't over do it. If volume is too high, we adjust. Communicate arm response after high days.
03
Self-Knowledge
Track how the arm feels each day. Build self-awareness around what makes you feel sharp vs flat. That knowledge is a weapon.
01
Isometric Foundation
Weeks 1–4 · Mon Lower A / Tue Upper A / Thu Lower B / Fri Upper C
Goal: Build joint stiffness and movement patterns under isometric load. Weeks 1–3 throw M/W/F building to 120ft. Week 4 shifts to 4-day throw schedule (M/T/T/F). Arm Care A on Tuesday, B on Friday.
