Return to Throw — Post Tommy John
AthleteGavin Maines
LevelCollege
SurgeryTommy John Reconstruction
BlockWeeks 1–16
Arm Health First: Everything in this program is designed around protecting the elbow. If anything feels off — sharpness, clicking, unexpected fatigue — stop immediately and communicate. Do not push through joint pain. The program can adjust. The surgery cannot be undone.
Arm Care Note: Arm care runs every Mon (A), Tue (B), Thu (A), Fri (B) — to feel. If volume is too high, communicate it and we adjust. Do not white-knuckle through it.
Goals & Program Structure
Return to Throw Goals
Full return to the mound — live BP and pen work by week 15–16
Arm health over velocity — mechanics and tissue tolerance come before output
Build a college-ready body — strength, stability, and arm care that can handle volume
Progressive throwing load — every week builds on the last, no skipping ahead
Block Structure
Weeks 1–6: Distance toss program — 25ft building to 90ft. 3x/week throwing.
Week 7: Deload — distance protocol, lighter volume
Week 8: Long toss + first mound work at 75% intensity
Weeks 9–13: Progressive mound work — 90% to 100%, full arsenal
Week 14: Deload — prepare the arm for live work
Weeks 15–16: Pen work, bullpen, and live BP — full return
Program Focus Areas
01
Arm Tolerance
The arm must be rebuilt gradually. Every throwing session has a purpose — no session is wasted, no session is skipped without communication.
02
Lift Foundation
Isometric phase builds joint stiffness and movement patterns. Eccentric phase accumulates tissue stress. College options are available — talk to your coach before adding.
03
Arm Care Protocol
Intensive arm care program runs alongside this throwing build. Do not over do it — the protocol is designed to complement the throwing load, not add to it.
