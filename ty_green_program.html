Rehab Development Program
AthleteTy Green
SchoolDavie HS — Senior
StatusTommy John Rehab
BlockWeeks 1–8
Tommy John Rehab — No Throwing: This block does not include any throwing. Arm recovery is the priority. The program focuses entirely on building a stronger, more resilient body so that when the arm is cleared, everything around it is ready. All throwing decisions are made with your medical team — not this program.
Flexible by Design: This program is a framework, not a mandate. Adjust volume and session timing based on how you feel. If something is too much — lower it and communicate. The program adapts. You lead.
Goals & Program Structure
Rehab Goals
Full recovery — let the arm heal completely before return to throwing
Come back stronger — build the body that supports the arm during this window
Lower half and athleticism — don't lose it while the arm heals
Arm care for strength — higher weight, less volume. Build the posterior cuff and scap.
Block Structure
4-day lift: Monday Lower A · Tuesday Upper A · Thursday Lower B · Friday Upper C
Wednesday off — rest and recovery
Arm Care A on Tuesday · Arm Care B on Friday — strength emphasis
Post workout: A on Mon + Thu · B on Tue + Fri
Program Focus Areas
01
Arm Care — Strength
Arm care A on Tuesday, B on Friday. Higher weight, less volume. Build the posterior cuff and scapular stability so the arm comes back into a stronger structure.
02
Lower Half Power
Split squats, lateral bounds, deadlifts — build the legs and hips. When you return to throwing, the power comes from the ground up. Don't waste this window.
03
Tissue Work
Tissue work is critical while on an intensive arm care program. Foam roll and lacrosse ball every session — before everything. Non-negotiable.
01
Isometric Foundation
Weeks 1–4 · Mon Lower A / Tue Upper A / Thu Lower B / Fri Upper C
Goal: Build joint stiffness and movement patterns under isometric load. Position and sustained tension are the stimulus. Learn to brace and hold — the eccentric work in Phase 2 demands this foundation. Arm care runs Tuesday and Friday throughout both phases.

02
Eccentric / Volume
Weeks 5–8 · Same 4-Day Schedule · Slow Eccentrics · Higher Volume
Goal: Accumulate tissue stress through controlled eccentric loading. The descent is the training stimulus — slow and deliberate every rep. Arm care continues the same schedule. Communicate any soreness or fatigue as volume increases.
