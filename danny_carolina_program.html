Goals & Program Structure
Player Goals
Sign to pro ball — develop into a draftable, signable position player
Be athletic — throw like a shortstop, move like one
Mix Jeter throws, front hand, back hand, rotation throws — own every angle
Build a pro-ready body — strength, power, and durability
Block Structure
Weeks 1–3: M/W/F throwing — building distance and plyo foundation
Week 4: Shifts to M/T/T/F throw schedule — 180–200ft range
Weeks 5–7: 200–220ft + pulldown phase begins week 6
Week 8: Deload — every day light/medium, arm adapts during rest
Program Focus Areas
01
Athletic Throwing
Shortstop mentality every session. Jeter throws, front hand, back hand, rotation throws — always adjustable to how you feel.
02
Lift Foundation
Phase 1 builds joint stiffness and movement quality. Phase 2 loads eccentrics. Both exist to make you a more explosive, durable athlete.
03
Recovery Built In
Post-throw A/B/C rotates through the week. If volume is too high, lower it — do not white-knuckle. Communicate and adjust.
01
Isometric Foundation
Weeks 1–4 · Mon Lower A / Tue Upper A / Thu Lower B / Fri Upper C
Goal: Build joint stiffness and movement patterns under isometric load. Weeks 1–3 throw M/W/F — week 4 shifts to M/T/T/F. Lift runs 4 days every week. Post-throw rotates A → B → C.
