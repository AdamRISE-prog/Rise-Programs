Pitcher Development Program
AthleteLevi Block
SchoolDavy — Sophomore
GoalsVelo · Command · Strength
BlockWeeks 1–9
Flexible by Design: This program is a framework, not a mandate. Lift days are ideally Mon/Wed/Fri but can shift to Tue or Thu — you do not have to do them Monday/Wednesday/Friday if it doesn't fit. Adjust intent, volume, and session timing based on how you feel. Communicate everything.
Goals & Program Structure
Player Goals
Gain velocity — develop real arm speed through training
Locate with velocity — command the velo, not just throw it
Gain more muscle — build a body that can sustain the arm
Be less tight — mobility and tissue quality every session
Block Structure
Week 1: M/W/F light — establishing the pattern at 90ft
Weeks 2–5: M/T/T/F — building bullpen work and catch distance
Week 6: Deload — back to M/W/F light. Mandatory reset.
Weeks 7–8: Pulldown phase — 3 then 5 pulldowns to 100ft+
Week 9: Pulldown mound phase — bullpen 15 + bullpen 20
Program Focus Areas
01
Velocity Building
Plyo progressions and pulldowns develop arm speed. Bullpen work teaches you to command the velo you build.
02
Mobility First
Prep protocol every session — couch stretch, pigeon, back bridge, slow hip switches. Being less tight is a training outcome, not an accident.
03
Arm Care
Arm Care A on Tuesday, B on Friday — to feel. Communicate if volume is too high. Do not white-knuckle through it.
01
Isometric Foundation
Weeks 1–5 · Lift A Mon / Lift B Wed / Lift C Fri · Flexible to Tue or Thu
Goal: Build joint stiffness and movement patterns. Week 1 throw M/W/F at 90ft. Weeks 2–5 shift to M/T/T/F with bullpen work building. Arm Care A on Tuesday, B on Friday. Lift days can shift — communicate if needed.
